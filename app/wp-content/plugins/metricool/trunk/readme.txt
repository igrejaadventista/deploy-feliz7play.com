== Description ==

Metricool brings powerful analytics, actionable statistics, and complete social media insights directly into your WordPress dashboard. With a simple setup, this plugin connects your website to Metricool and automatically inserts the tracking JavaScript needed to collect accurate site analytics and statistics—no complicated configuration required.

Once connected, you can monitor your website performance alongside your social media analytics in one unified place. Track visits, user behavior, and key statistics from your WordPress site, while also gaining insights into your Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn, and X (Twitter) accounts. Metricool is designed for creators, businesses, and social media managers who want clear, visual analytics without the complexity.

The plugin adds a lightweight tracking script to your front-end, enabling precise website analytics similar to tools like Google Analytics, but with a strong focus on social media performance and cross-channel statistics. Inside wp-admin, your data is presented in clean, easy-to-understand graphs, making it simple to analyze trends, measure growth, and optimize your social strategy.

Getting started is effortless thanks to the built-in onboarding flow. You can quickly create a free Metricool account or connect an existing one, allowing you to start collecting analytics and statistics immediately. From there, you’ll have access to a complete overview of your social media and website performance in one place.

Metricool supports a wide range of social media platforms, advertising channels, and reporting tools, allowing you to centralize all your analytics and statistics:

* Instagram
* Facebook
* YouTube
* LinkedIn
* X (Twitter)
* Threads
* Bluesky
* Pinterest
* TikTok
* TikTok Business
* Google Business Profile
* Twitch
* Meta Ads
* Google Ads
* TikTok Ads
* Looker Studio

Whether you’re tracking Instagram engagement, analyzing Facebook reach, optimizing TikTok performance, monitoring YouTube views, reviewing LinkedIn statistics, or understanding your X (Twitter) audience, Metricool centralizes all your analytics and statistics into a single, intuitive dashboard.

If you’re familiar with tools like Google Analytics or plugins like Smash, Metricool offers a more integrated approach—combining website analytics with social media analytics and advertising statistics for a complete picture of your digital performance.

== Installation ==

1. Upload `metricool.zip` via WordPress -> Plugins -> Add New or the unzipped file via FTP to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory
2. Activate the plugin through the Plugins menu in WordPress
3. Follow the short plugin onboarding and immediately enjoy useful site and social media analytics!

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Is Metricool free to use? =
Yes, the plugin and registering an account at Metricool.com is entirely free, without the need to add credit card information. You can gather metrics from your site and multiple social media accounts without the need to upgrade.

= Do I need to register for an account? =
Yes, we need to authenticate your site as your site statistics will be processed through our API. This allows us to provide you with the best features. The plugin will assist you through the short onboarding process. Alternatively, you can register an account at https://metricool.com

= What if I already have a Metricool account? =
You can skip the onboarding and login with your existing login credentials.
